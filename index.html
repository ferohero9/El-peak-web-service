          Questions people ask

          Who owns the website when it is finished?

          You do. Every source file is handed over at completion, and the site keeps running on your hosting whether or not we stay in touch.

          Is hosting included in the EGP 1,500?

          No. The build is a one-time fee of EGP 1,500. Hosting and the domain are EGP 500 a year, billed separately.

          How does payment work?

          Directly to El-Peak, either in full or across two milestones agreed before starting. All prices are in Egyptian Pounds.

          What if I need changes after launch?

          On-demand updates are EGP 400 per request. One request covers photo swaps, price and text updates, banner replacement, or adding and removing up to five items. You can also edit the files yourself; they are yours.

          Can I get an admin panel to edit the site myself?

          Not yet. The dynamic admin portal and backend is listed at EGP 5,000 and marked Coming soon. It cannot be ordered today.

          How long does a build take?

          Turnaround depends on the scope of the page and how ready your content is at kickoff. I confirm a start date and the review steps in our first conversation, before any payment is agreed.
