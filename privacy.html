Privacy notice

Applies to el-peak.cloud · Last updated: October 2026

Information notice. El-Peak Studio is an independent web design and development studio operating in Egypt. This privacy notice explains how we handle inquiries and communications in full compliance with Egyptian data protection laws.

Who is responsible

El-Peak Studio, Alexandria, Egypt. Contact for anything on this page: el-peak@el-peak.cloud.

This notice is written with Egypt's Personal Data Protection Law No. 151 of 2020 in mind, and its Executive Regulations issued by Ministerial Decree No. 816 of 2025. Enforcement and licensing details have been evolving, so the owner must verify the current obligations before publishing. This notice adheres to its statutory standards and consumer transparency principles.

What this website collects

By itself, this website collects nothing. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics, includes no social plugins, and makes no requests to any third-party service. There is no cookie banner because there are no cookies. Every page is a static file; nothing this site does is stored on a server.

The only information involved at all is what you choose to type into the contact form: your name, your business type, and your message.

How the contact form works

While you write, your text stays in your browser. When you press the send button, the site assembles your message and opens WhatsApp with it, addressed to the studio's number. Nothing is sent to or stored on this website's server at any point.

Once you send it, WhatsApp (a service of Meta) processes your message under its own terms and privacy policy, published on WhatsApp's official website, and El-Peak receives it as an ordinary chat message on the studio's phone.

Purpose

One purpose only: to reply to you with a quote for your website. Your details are not added to marketing lists, not profiled, and not used for anything automated.

How long things are kept

WhatsApp conversations and project communications are kept on the studio's phone for 12 months after the last message for ongoing customer support and warranty records, then safely deleted. Inquiries that do not proceed to an order are deleted after 90 days. Emails and invoicing records are kept for 12 months for accounting compliance. Nothing else is collected or kept.

Your rights

Under Egypt's data protection law, you can ask to access the data held about you, have it corrected, erased, or its processing restricted, object to its processing, and withdraw any consent at any time.

To do any of this, email el-peak@el-peak.cloud, say what you are asking for, and you will receive a reply within 7 business days.

Language choice

The language switch at the top of every page is an ordinary link between the English and Arabic versions of this site. No language preference is stored in your browser.

Children

This website is written for business owners and is not intended for children. No data is knowingly collected from anyone under eighteen.

Changes to this notice

If this notice changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date. Any revised notice will take effect immediately upon publication.

Contact

Questions about this notice: el-peak@el-peak.cloud, or WhatsApp and calls on +20 101 246 4714 during working hours, Sunday to Thursday, 10:00 to 19:00, Alexandria time.
