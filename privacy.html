3. Hosting Infrastructure & Technical Server Logs (GitHub Pages)

To ensure high performance, security, and uptime, this website is hosted as a static project site utilizing GitHub Pages, a cloud hosting service provided by GitHub, Inc. (an affiliate of Microsoft Corporation, 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA):

  • Automated Infrastructure Logging: When your web browser requests files from this website, GitHub’s edge servers automatically log standard network transmission records. These records typically include your Internet Protocol (IP) address, requested Uniform Resource Identifier (URI), access timestamp, HTTP referrer header, HTTP status code, and browser User-Agent string.
  • Purpose and Legal Grounds: These technical logs are collected by GitHub to safeguard server infrastructure, prevent automated abuse and Distributed Denial-of-Service (DDoS) attacks, ensure network availability, and comply with cybersecurity obligations. Under Egyptian Law No. 151 of 2020, processing of necessary technical identifiers for network security constitutes a recognized legitimate operational ground.
  • Separation of Control & Absence of Backend Database: El-Peak Studio operates this website exclusively as a static repository of static HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and image assets. El-Peak Studio has no administrative access to GitHub’s raw infrastructure access logs, does not store visitor IP addresses on any private server, maintains no database of web visitors, and cannot independently alter, rotate, or delete GitHub’s internal security logs.
  • Third-Party Privacy Disclosures: For comprehensive details on how GitHub processes technical traffic data, please consult the official GitHub Privacy Statement.

5. Client-Side Form & Quotation Estimator Architecture

Our website features an interactive project brief composer and cost estimator tool. Their technical operation is completely transparent:

  • Exclusively Local Processing: While you type into the name, business type, or project description fields on our contact form, all text input and calculation logic execute entirely within your local device’s browser memory (DOM). No data is transmitted to, cached on, or processed by any remote web server or backend API during form entry.
  • Voluntary WhatsApp Handoff: When you click “Open in WhatsApp” or “Send this list on WhatsApp,” the browser encodes your entered text into a standard URL query string (using https://wa.me/201012464714?text=...) and launches your local WhatsApp client or web interface. No transmission occurs until you deliberately review and click send within WhatsApp itself.
  • No-JavaScript Fallback: If JavaScript is disabled, the form performs a standard HTTP GET request directly to WhatsApp’s web endpoint, ensuring identical client-side routing without server-side intermediate retention.

6. Cookies, Local Browser Storage & External Resources

We respect your privacy by omitting tracking and surveillance technologies:

  • No Tracking Cookies: This website places zero advertising, tracking, behavioral analytics, or third-party profiling cookies on your computer or mobile device.
  • No Cookie Banner Required: Because no non-essential cookies or tracker scripts are executed, no intrusive cookie consent banner is mandated under Egyptian Personal Data Protection Law No. 151 of 2020.
  • Local Browser Storage in Demonstrations: In our interactive restaurant demonstration template (Al-Noor Kitchen), the client-side cart script uses the browser’s standard localStorage API (under the key alnoor-cart) solely to preserve menu items in the cart across page reloads on your device. This storage is strictly local to your browser, is never transmitted to our studio or any third party, and can be cleared at any time via your browser settings.
  • Self-Hosted Assets: All stylesheets, vanilla scripts, SVG brand icons, and typography are bundled locally within this repository. We do not load remote Google Fonts, Cloudflare CDNs, Meta Pixels, or external web beacons, preventing third parties from monitoring your browsing behavior across the web.

We adhere strictly to the statutory standards governing direct electronic marketing under Egyptian law:

Prior opt-in consent alone does not legally authorize direct electronic marketing in the absence of the statutory permit.

10. Data Retention & Secure Disposal Schedule

We maintain a clear distinction between mandatory statutory retention obligations and internal operational guidelines (Article 2(5) of Decree No. 816 of 2025):

  • Statutory Tax and Accounting Records (Article 38, Law No. 206 of 2020): Under the third paragraph of Article 38 of the Egyptian Unified Tax Procedures Law No. 206 of 2020 (whose first paragraph was amended by Law No. 150 of 2026 without altering this paragraph), relevant tax records, books of account, invoice copies, and payment confirmations must be retained for five years following the tax period for which the declaration is submitted (خمس سنوات تالية للفترة الضريبية التي يُقدم عنها الإقرار). This statutory requirement applies strictly to tax-relevant accounting and financial documents rather than automatically encompassing all categories of personal data; ordinary inquiries and pre-contractual communications remain subject to their separate operational retention schedules described below.
  • Operational Target for Unconverted Inquiries: Prospective quotation requests and general inquiries that do not result in a commissioned contract are subject to an internal operational target of deletion within ninety (90) calendar days from the date of last communication. This is a manual operational practice conducted periodically by the proprietor across messaging devices and email inboxes, rather than an automated server-side technical process.
  • Operational Target for Client Project Files: Project briefs, technical correspondence, custom code archives, and handover receipts are subject to an internal operational retention guideline of twelve (12) months following deployment and handover. This allows the studio to support the voluntary technical warranty and facilitate requested updates. Following twelve (12) months, correspondence and working files are archived or manually deleted, unless ongoing maintenance is agreed.
  • Disposal Protocol: Expired local data files are permanently deleted and overwritten on local devices.

12. Technical Security & Confidentiality Measures

In fulfillment of Article 2(7) of Ministerial Decree No. 816 of 2025, security safeguards are implemented across two distinct layers:

  • Web Hosting Transit Layer: Web traffic to this website is served over Hypertext Transfer Protocol Secure (HTTPS) with automated Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.2/1.3) certificates managed by GitHub Pages infrastructure, preventing transit tampering.
  • Static Architecture Advantage: Because our website contains no server-side executable code, no dynamic database, and no administrative user portal, it is inherently immune to SQL injection attacks, database credential theft, and server-side script breaches.
  • Operator Device Safeguards: Client communications and project source files stored on studio hardware are subject to internal operational security practices applied by the proprietor, including strong device passcodes, biometric locks, and local full-disk encryption (such as BitLocker/FileVault).
  • Realistic Security Acknowledgment: While these proportionate precautions are applied, no internet transmission or electronic storage medium is completely invulnerable. The studio makes no misleading declarations of absolute, breach-proof security.

13. Personal Data Breach Management (Decree No. 816 of 2025, Article 5)

In strict compliance with Article 5 of the Executive Regulations (Ministerial Decree No. 816 of 2025), we maintain an established incident response protocol in the event of any security incident compromising personal data:

  • 72-Hour Regulatory Notification: In the event of a verified personal data breach resulting in unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or loss of personal data held by the studio, we will notify the Egyptian Personal Data Protection Center (PDPC) within seventy-two (72) hours of becoming aware of the incident.
  • Content of Notification: The regulatory report will detail the nature of the breach, the categories and estimated number of affected data subjects, likely consequences, and corrective remediation measures executed.
  • Notification to Affected Data Subjects within Three Business Days: Pursuant to Article 5 of Ministerial Decree No. 816 of 2025, in all cases of a verified personal data breach, we will notify the affected data subjects directly within three (3) business days from the date of notifying the Personal Data Protection Center, providing detailed particulars regarding the nature of the breach, its potential impacts, and the corrective and preventive measures undertaken.
  • Breach Documentation: In accordance with Article 5(4) of Decree No. 816 of 2025, we maintain a secure internal register documenting all security incidents, their factual context, impacts, and remedial actions taken.

14. Your Statutory Rights Under Egyptian Law

Under Chapter 3 of Egypt’s Personal Data Protection Law No. 151 of 2020 and its Executive Regulations, you are endowed with comprehensive, legally enforceable rights:

  • Right of Access & Knowledge (Article 2): You have the right to know what personal data relating to you is held by our studio, to be informed of the purposes of processing, and to receive a clear copy thereof.
  • Right of Rectification (Article 2): You have the right to request the prompt correction, updating, or completion of inaccurate or outdated personal records.
  • Right of Erasure (“Right to be Forgotten”): You have the right to demand the permanent deletion of your personal data when the processing purpose has expired, when consent is revoked, or when processing contravenes statutory standards.
  • Right of Restriction & Objection: You have the right to object to or request the limitation of processing operations that conflict with your legitimate rights or applicable law.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You have the right to withdraw previously granted consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing conducted prior to revocation.

How to Exercise Your Rights: To submit a statutory data protection request, please email our studio directly at el-peak@el-peak.cloud. We will verify your identity and respond to legitimate requests free of charge within seven (7) business days. You also retain the right to submit grievances directly to the Egyptian Personal Data Protection Center (PDPC) if you believe your statutory rights have been compromised.
