2. Technical Infrastructure & Hosting Environment (cPanel)

This website comprises lightweight, static frontend assets (HTML5, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript) hosted on a standard Linux shared hosting environment operated via cPanel. In accordance with standard web server protocols:

  • Automated Server Access Logs: When your web browser requests pages from this site, the underlying web server (Apache/LiteSpeed within cPanel) automatically records standard HTTP access log entries. These technical records typically include your truncated or full Internet Protocol (IP) address, requested URI path, timestamp, HTTP referral header, HTTP response status code, and browser user-agent string.
  • Purpose and Legal Basis: These logs are generated automatically for system administration, infrastructure defense (e.g. rate limiting and DDoS mitigation), identifying broken links (HTTP 404/500 errors), and ensuring network security. Processing is grounded in our legitimate operational interest to maintain infrastructure integrity and statutory cybersecurity duties under Egyptian law.
  • Server Log Retention: Standard cPanel access logs are managed according to the web host's log-rotation schedule (typically retained for 30 to 90 days), after which they are permanently overwritten or purged. Server logs are never cross-referenced with contact messages or used for behavioral profiling.

3. Cookies, Tracking & Third-Party Scripts

We believe in privacy by design and technological restraint:

  • No Tracking Cookies: This website does not set any tracking, profiling, marketing, or third-party cookies.
  • No Third-Party CDNs or Pixels: We do not load Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, tracking beacons, or remote font CDNs. All typography, CSS stylesheets, icons, and JavaScript files are self-hosted directly from our own domain server.
  • No Cookie Banner Required: Because zero non-essential cookies or tracker scripts are executed, no intrusive cookie consent banner is mandated under Egyptian Personal Data Protection Law No. 151/2020.

4. Client Inquiries & WhatsApp Communication

Our website provides an interactive quotation generator and brief composer:

  • Client-Side Execution: While typing into our contact brief form, all data processing occurs entirely client-side within your browser memory (DOM). No data is transmitted to, cached on, or processed by our cPanel web server during entry.
  • Handoff to WhatsApp: Upon clicking the “Open in WhatsApp” action, the browser constructs a pre-formatted URI string containing your provided name, business category, and inquiry summary, and directs your device to the official WhatsApp interface (Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd. / Meta Platforms Inc.).
  • Third-Party Processing: Once transmitted via WhatsApp, communication is encrypted end-to-end and subject to WhatsApp’s global Privacy Policy. El-Peak Studio receives your message as a direct chat communication on our dedicated business device.

5. Purposes of Processing & Legal Ground

We process personal data solely under recognized lawful bases under Law No. 151 of 2020:

  • Pre-contractual & Contractual Steps (Article 5): Processing your contact name, business scope, phone number, and project requirements to prepare quotation estimates, scope project milestones, and execute website development agreements.
  • Statutory Compliance: Archiving commercial invoices, payment milestone acknowledgments, and tax records in fulfillment of Egyptian commercial accounting regulations.
  • No Secondary Monetization: We do not sell, rent, monetize, or disclose your contact details or business information to third-party data brokers or marketing agencies.

6. Data Retention Schedule

Personal data is retained strictly as long as necessary for legitimate business records and legal defense:

  • Completed Client Projects: Project communication threads, specifications, and handover manifests are retained for twelve (12) months following final website deployment for warranty service, updates, and customer support, after which they are securely wiped.
  • Unconverted Inquiries: Prospective quotation requests that do not proceed to an active contract are purged after ninety (90) calendar days.
  • Financial and Transaction Records: Invoices, milestone receipts, and tax records are retained for the statutory period mandated by Egyptian tax authorities.

7. Your Statutory Rights Under Egyptian Law

Under Chapter 3 of Egypt’s Personal Data Protection Law No. 151 of 2020, data subjects enjoy comprehensive legal protections, including:

  • Right of Access & Knowledge: The right to be informed whether your personal data is held and to receive a clear copy thereof.
  • Right of Rectification: The right to demand correction, completion, or updating of inaccurate personal data.
  • Right of Erasure (“Right to be Forgotten”): The right to request the deletion of your personal records once the initial processing purpose has elapsed.
  • Right of Restriction & Objection: The right to object to or restrict processing operations that conflict with your statutory rights.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: The right to revoke consent previously granted for optional communications at any time without retroactive penalty.

To exercise any of these statutory rights, please submit a written request to el-peak@el-peak.cloud. We will verify your identity and fulfill valid statutory requests within seven (7) business days, free of charge.

8. International Data Transfers & Hosting Architecture

While our business and management reside in Alexandria, Egypt, our web server infrastructure utilizes Tier-3 data center facilities with cPanel management located in compliant jurisdictions. All data transmissions between your browser and our server are secured with high-grade Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.2/1.3, HTTPS) encryption. In compliance with Article 14 of Law No. 151/2020, cross-border technical hosting maintains rigorous security protocols and confidentiality measures matching Egyptian statutory safeguards.
