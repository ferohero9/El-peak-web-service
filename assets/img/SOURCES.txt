SHOT LIST · production spec
- Aspect ratio: 4:3 desktop crop. Mobile art-directed crop: 3:4, centered on
  the subject, saved as the -m-480 variants.
- Widths: 480w, 800w, 1200w. Formats: AVIF + WebP + JPEG fallback.
- Budget: 800w WebP under 120 KB each; 1200w WebP under 200 KB; JPEG
  fallback roughly 1.5x the WebP size.
- Alt text for each photo is already written in both index pages; adjust it
  if the final photo differs from the described scene.

1. demo-restaurant — warm light, food-focused: a working grill with flames,
   skewers over coals, or a top-down mezze table. Depth and texture over
   people. Search: "restaurant kitchen grill", "kebab grill close",
   "mezze table top view". Avoid: chefs' faces, readable menus, branded
   packaging, alcohol in frame.
2. demo-electronics — tidy commerce: shop counter with boxed accessories,
   glass shelving, or a repair bench with tools laid out. Search: "phone
   repair bench tools", "mobile shop shelves", "electronics counter
   display". Avoid: visible phone brands/logos, screens showing branded
   UIs, price tags with readable text.
3. demo-clinic — bright, empty, calm: treatment chair, overhead lamp,
   instrument tray, clean surfaces. Search: "dental clinic room empty",
   "dentist chair lamp", "dental treatment room bright". Avoid: patients,
   close-up instruments in mouths, x-rays, equipment brand names.
4. demo-contractor — honest work in progress: metal stud framing, scaffolding
   inside a shell, or fresh plasterwork with trowel marks. Search: "interior
   fit out framing", "drywall construction interior", "plastering wall
   progress". Avoid: faces, signage, address plates, exposed wiring drama.
