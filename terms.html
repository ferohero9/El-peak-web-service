Terms of service

Applies to work ordered from El-Peak Studio · Last updated: October 2026

Commercial terms. These terms outline the working relationship between El-Peak Studio and our clients for website design, development, and deployment services.

What you buy

A complete static website of one custom page:

  • Single-page custom design, responsive and mobile first
  • WhatsApp ordering and enquiry integration
  • Category filtering and out-of-stock controls
  • Map location, social links, click-to-call
  • Deployment to standard cPanel shared hosting
  • Full source files handed over at completion

Fee and payment

The build fee is EGP 1,500, one time. Payment goes directly to El-Peak, either in full or across two milestones agreed in writing before work starts. Payments are split into two equal milestones: a 50% deposit before design and development commence, and the final 50% balance upon final project review and deployment to your hosting.

All prices are in Egyptian Pounds. All prices are quoted net in Egyptian Pounds (EGP).

Revisions

Each build includes up to two (2) comprehensive rounds of revisions prior to final delivery. Revisions cover adjustments to styling, layout hierarchy, color adjustments, image swaps, and copy polishing. New feature requests, structural redesigns, or additions beyond the agreed scope are quoted as additional work or covered under standard update requests.

Delivery and ownership

At completion you receive every source file for your website. Ownership of the files passes to you on final payment. Full copyright, code ownership, and usage rights for the bespoke HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and delivered configuration files transfer to you unconditionally upon receipt of final payment.

Your website keeps working on your hosting whether or not we stay in contact.

Third-party assets

Any stock photographs used in your build are licensed for your website and the licence passes to you. Fonts are open-licence (SIL Open Font License) and remain under that licence, which permits commercial use. A comprehensive asset inventory documenting all open licenses (such as SIL OFL fonts) and licensed media is included with every project handover package.

Hosting and domain

Hosting and domain are EGP 500 per year, billed separately from the build. Hosting is a third-party service with its own terms; El-Peak deploys your site to standard cPanel shared hosting but is not the hosting provider. The EGP 500 annual fee covers provisioning and renewal of standard shared cPanel hosting and domain registration. Domains are registered directly in your name or business entity, guaranteeing that you retain complete autonomous ownership.

Content updates

On-demand updates are EGP 400 per request. One request covers swapping photos, updating prices and text, replacing banners, or adding and removing up to five items. You can also edit the files yourself at any time; they are yours.

The dynamic admin portal and backend (EGP 5,000) is a future release. It cannot be ordered today, and nothing on this site should be read as offering it.

Working days are Sunday to Thursday, 10:00 to 19:00, Alexandria time. Messages receive a reply within one business day.
