Applies to all projects executed by El-Peak Studio · Effective Date: October 2026

Contractual Transparency: These terms define the professional rights, deliverables, milestone payment structure, intellectual property transfer, and liability boundaries governing custom static website builds provided by El-Peak Studio.

1. Scope of Deliverables

El-Peak Studio specializes in handcrafted, high-speed single-page static websites tailored for local Egyptian enterprises. Each commissioned website build comprises:

  • Bespoke Frontend Engineering: Lightweight, semantic HTML5, modern CSS3, and vanilla JavaScript without cumbersome frameworks, dependencies, or database vulnerabilities.
  • Mobile-First & Responsive Layout: Fully fluid interface verified across mobile, tablet, and desktop viewports, complete with accessible navigation and dark/light system adaptation.
  • Direct Customer Conversion Pathways: WhatsApp business messaging integration with custom pre-filled message builders, interactive order sheets or appointment request pickers, click-to-call links, and geographic location links.
  • Optimized Performance Assets: Self-hosted, open-licensed fonts (SIL OFL) and optimized media assets configured for high-speed local loading on Egyptian mobile telecommunications networks.
  • Deployment to Shared Hosting (cPanel): Professional deployment directly to your standard Linux shared hosting environment via cPanel or FTP, with server-level redirects, security headers, and compression enabled in .htaccess.
  • Complete Handover Package: Delivery of the unminified source code repository and production archive directly to your custody.

2. Pricing & Milestone Payment Structure

Commercial clarity is fundamental to our service agreements:

  • One-Time Build Fee: The baseline one-time production fee is fixed at EGP 1,500 per custom static website, covering the scope detailed above.
  • 50 / 50 Milestone Split: Payment is strictly structured across two transparent stages:
    1. Initial Deposit (50% — EGP 750): Invoiced and payable prior to commencement of design drafts, asset preparation, and development work.
    2. Final Handover Milestone (50% — EGP 750): Invoiced and payable upon project completion, staging approval, and deployment to the client’s live cPanel hosting.
  • Currency & Taxes: All quotations and fees are quoted in Egyptian Pounds (EGP). All agreed prices are net of bank transfer or mobile wallet transaction processing fees.
  • Payment Methods: Payments are accepted via direct Egyptian bank transfer (InstaPay / ACH) or verified Egyptian mobile cash wallets (Vodafone Cash, Orange Cash, etc.) to the studio’s verified business accounts.

3. Review & Revision Rounds

To ensure collaborative satisfaction while preserving project velocity:

  • Included Revision Rounds: Each project build includes up to two (2) comprehensive rounds of revisions prior to final sign-off.
  • Permissible Revisions: Revisions encompass adjustments to visual hierarchy, color palette tuning, typography scale, text copy refinement, photo swaps, and form placeholder wording within the agreed single-page layout.
  • Out-of-Scope Alterations: Major structural changes outside the agreed initial brief—such as converting into a multi-page portal, adding external third-party API integrations, or switching overall business scope post-approval—are quoted separately or billed as on-demand content updates.

4. Handover & Absolute Intellectual Property Ownership

We do not lock clients into proprietary site-builder platforms:

  • Unconditional Code Ownership: Upon receipt of the final milestone payment, full, unconditional ownership and copyright in the custom HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and delivered configuration files pass directly and permanently to you.
  • Independence: Your static website operates autonomously on your cPanel hosting. It does not phone home, require proprietary subscriptions, or depend on ongoing contact with our studio to remain fully operational.

5. Third-Party Licenses & Open Fonts

All assets incorporated into your deliverable respect international licensing laws:

  • Open-Source Typography: Embedded fonts utilize recognized open licenses (such as the SIL Open Font License - OFL), permitting perpetual commercial use without royalty fees.
  • Stock Photography: Any commercial stock photography supplied by El-Peak Studio is licensed for commercial web display on your site. You receive an asset manifest detailing font sources and media licenses upon handover.

6. Domain Names & cPanel Hosting Terms

Transparency regarding the technical operating environment:

  • Direct Autonomous Ownership: The annual hosting and domain package (EGP 500 / year) covers standard shared Linux cPanel hosting provisioning and domain registration (.com, .net, etc.). Domains are registered in the client’s or business’s name, ensuring you retain legal authority over your domain.
  • cPanel Provider Relationship: El-Peak Studio configures, optimizes, and uploads your static files to the cPanel hosting account. However, underlying server availability, DNS resolution speeds, electrical uptime, and data center connectivity remain governed by the respective third-party hosting infrastructure provider’s Service Level Agreement (SLA).
  • Client-Supplied Hosting: If you already possess your own hosting environment or cPanel account, we deploy directly to your existing server at no extra surcharge.

7. On-Demand Content Updates

Website updates are straightforward and unbundled:

  • Predictable Flat Fee: Post-handover content updates are available on-demand at EGP 400 per batch request, without monthly lock-in contracts.
  • Scope of a Batch Update: A single update request covers editing text copy, adjusting product prices, updating menu items, swapping up to five (5) promotional images, or revising operating hours and phone numbers.
  • Self-Maintenance: Because all files are clean, readable static source code, you or any technical colleague can modify, upload, or backup files directly through cPanel File Manager or FTP at any time.

The client retains sole responsibility for:

  • Providing accurate commercial details, product menus, service descriptions, prices, phone numbers, and physical location coordinates.
  • Ensuring that all supplied trade names, logos, customer claims, health statements, and product listings comply with Egyptian consumer protection regulations (Consumer Protection Law No. 181 of 2018) and trademark laws.
  • Safeguarding client-side cPanel credentials and administrative email accounts.
